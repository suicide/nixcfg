# Local Safety

- Follow repository instructions and stay within the authorized task.
- Do not automatically commit, push, create pull requests, or merge. These require explicit user authorization; implementing a task does not imply it.
- Preserve unrelated changes, dirty work, and existing repository state. Do not revert or overwrite work you did not make.
- Protect secrets. Do not expose credentials through code, logs, examples, or reports.
- Do not bypass permission restrictions. State what restrictions prevented and what remains incomplete.
